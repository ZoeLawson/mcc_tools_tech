What to know about French Bulldog

The French bulldog (Frenchie) is a small, muscular dog with their trademark bat-like ears.

Along with their adorable looks, Frenchies are lively and lovable.

  • Height: 11 to 13 inches.
  • Weight: 19 to 28 pounds.
  • Coat: Short, smooth.
  • Coat Color: Brindle, fawn, white.

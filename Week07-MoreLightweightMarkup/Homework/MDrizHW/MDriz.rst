
Crock Pot versus Instant Pot
############################

Aunt Betty is interested in creating hearty meals that required minimum supervision. 

	I have researched the pros and cons of two cookware possibilities that she may be interested.
	
Crock Pot Pros
**************

* Easy to use, set it and forget it with one-touch dial

	- More affordable
	
	+ Excellent for dishes that required low temperature cooking.
	
	- Great for stews and pulled pork.
	
Instant Pot Pros
================

#. Foods cooks significantly faster.

A. Pressure cooking function

	a. This function can create tender meats in minutes. 
	
+-------------------------+------------------------+
| Cons Crock Pot          | Cons Instant Pot       |
+=========================+========================+
| Long Cooking Times      | Steep learning curve   |
+-------------------------+------------------------+
| Lack of Browning        | Space and Size         |
+-------------------------+------------------------+
| Texture Issues          | Cleaning Challenges    |
+-------------------------+------------------------+

.. image:: crock pot.png

My Recommendation
-----------------

**Crock Pot** 

	*Best for budget-conscious cooks who enjoys preparing simple, hearty meals slowly*

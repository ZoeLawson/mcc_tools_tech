Flare content is just HTML. For basic content, you can just use the Flare editor to insert HTML. If you're familiar with HTML, a lot of Flare will be familiar.

A lot of the great features of Flare, such as conditions, snippets, and variables, are specific elements, defined in the MadCap namespace. Until you know what you're doing, use Flare to add in the extra bits.

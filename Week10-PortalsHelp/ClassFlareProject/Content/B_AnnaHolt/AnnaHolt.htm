How to Join the Greater Boston ADHD+ Adult Meetup Group's Discord Server

Overview

The Greater Boston ADHD+ Meetup Group has used Discord to keep in touch between monthly Zoom meetings. Discord is a popular messaging app that allows for groups to communicate with each other.

We use it for all of the following:

<ul>

<li> To post links to resources </li>

<li> To request body-doubling or other peer support to complete tasks </li>

<li> To share other events of interest to individuals with ADHD </li>

<li> To share memes </li>

<li> To chat and build community </li>

Discord is available both on the web and as an app. Users will appear by default as the unique username they signed up with at the time. Your organizer (Anna) provides a link each month to join the group.

Instructions for Creating a Discord Username

To create a username on discord.com:

  1. 1. Visit discord.com and click on "Open Discord in Your Browser."

    <i>Tip</i>: this will also be how you log in with your username once you have created it!

  2. Click on "Register" at the bottom of the login page.

  3. Select a unique username and fill out the information required.

    <i>Tip </I>: This will be your master username across all affinity groups you may join. Each affinity group owns what is called a "server." Keep this in mind when selecting a username. It is possible to change your username for each group that you join, so you can go by your own name in the ADHD group server without having to have a personally identifying username.

  4. Complete the associated captcha and click verify.

Joining the Discord Server

Joining the Adult ADHD+ Meetup Group's Server can be done upon first

  1. Click "Have an invite already? Join a server."

  2. Input the unique code provided by the organizers for the Greater ADHD+ Meetup Group

    <i>Tip </i>: These links are only good for seven days after a meetup. If you would like to join the group meetup between regularly scheduled sessions, DM the organizer on Meetup.com for a personalized link.

  3. Once you have been taken to the server, you also be asked to verify your email.

    Go to the email address you provided at signup. Open the Discord email and click "Verify Email." You will be taken back to the Discord server.

  4. Click on the "Welcome and Rules" Channel.

    Each area with a #name is called a channel. Channels represent different topic areas or content.

    Once you are in the Greater Boston ADHD+ Meetup Group's Discord Server, please read the user rules. The user rules are posted at the top of the #welcome-and-rules channel. Scroll up to the top of the channel. Read all rules and confirm you are comfortable abiding by them. (These rules are also listed below if you don't want to scroll up!)

    Rules of the Boston Adult ADHD+ Meetup Group Description of the Rules
    Respect Others Don't make any offensive or overly personal comments, especially about gender identity/expression, sexual orientation, physical appearance, body size, age, race or religion. And, of course, absolutely no hate speech!
    Don't Out Anyone No real names, or identifying info about anyone, should be used in chat channels— or communicated in any way to others unless the person explicitly tells you it’s okay.
    Make it Accessible Write image descriptions when you post a photo, and link or post videos with captions and/or transcripts. Please be open and willing to repeat and rephrase for people.
    Honor Boundaries Sometimes topics will come up that are distressing and/or triggering for other members, such as abuse, dieting, or traumatic experiences. In order to maintain this as a welcoming space for everyone, please use Discord's spoiler markup with a vague descriptor as a content warning when discussing sensitive topics in text chat. To use spoiler markup, put two bars on either side of the spoiler, as so: ||spoiler here||
    SFW For the time being this is a safe for work server. If you must discuss sexually explicit topics, do so in the off-topic channel. Use spoiler tags and put NSFW at the start. Anyone who violates this rule once will be prohibited from posting to the server for 1 hr. 2nd offense, 1 day. 3rd offense, you will be kicked from the server.

  5. After this, you are free to start chatting. We look forward to having you!

    Please post something in the channel itself that introduces yourself. This does not have to be stressful! We are simply confirming you are a real human and not a bot!
